# Monetization hypothesis

This document records a hypothesis, not buyer, demand, payment, revenue, usage, or market proof.

## Hypothesis

A small contractor could value a private pre-submission evidence check when one preventable bid
rejection costs much more than a lightweight tool. A future paid version might bundle local batch
checks, team evidence retention, or jurisdiction-specific checklist templates.

## Why payment is deliberately absent

The current experiment needs to prove that the one-screen identifier check is understandable and
useful before accounts, billing, hosted storage, collaboration, or legal-policy content are added.
Those features would increase data, security, support, and compliance obligations.

## Evidence required before expansion

- Direct interviews with reachable estimators or bid coordinators.
- Observed use on synthetic or safely redacted packages.
- Evidence that exact identifier checking is not already reliably handled by the user's current
  checklist or procurement platform.
- Willingness-to-pay evidence tied to this narrow outcome.
- A fresh semantic-uniqueness review before changing the job or surface.

Until that evidence exists, monetization remains unvalidated.
